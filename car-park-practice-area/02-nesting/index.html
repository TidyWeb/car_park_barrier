Car parkbarrier

▸The rules

First hour free. Each hour after that costs £2. Permit holders pay nothing. A vehicle over 2 metres is refused and sent to the coach park; a refused vehicle pays nothing.

▸Saturday's facts

Seven cars came through. The blue Astra, LT19 KFN, was in for four hours, had no permit, and was 1.4 metres high. The council van, YB70 XCE, stood 2.4 metres high and was in for six hours with no permit. PK21 RAV, a red Fiat, stayed exactly one hour, has no permit and is 1.6 metres high. The silver Volvo, MC22 LEE, stayed nine hours, has a permit and is 1.8 metres high. DA18 NQW is 2.0 metres high, had no permit and stayed three hours. The black Mini, HF20 JET, was in for two hours, has no permit and is 1.5 metres high. Finally, RS71 OAK was in five hours, has no permit and is 1.9 metres high.

One car is deliberately described out of the neat order you might want. Sort the facts out before you code.

✓Job list

  1. Decide how to hold the day's cars and build it by hand. A list of cars or cars keyed by registration both work. Before writing code, add a comment saying which you chose and why.
  2. Print one line per car: registration, hours, and what it owes, to two decimal places.
  3. Refused cars say so instead of a charge, and say where to go.
  4. Print total takings for the day.
  5. Print how many cars were refused.
  6. Print the registration of the car that owed the most.
  7. What did LT19 KFN owe? Answer it from your structure, not by reading an earlier output line.

Height and charging are independent facts. A tall car can also have overstayed, but refusal means it must not count towards takings.

✱Before you run

Say the jobs in plain English first: hold the cars, visit each one, decide refusal, decide charge, keep the answers that grow, then ask one car a later question.

Choice A — list of dictionaries

Best for walking the whole day. Job 7 needs another loop to find LT19 KFN, which is its cost. 

cars = [
    {"reg": "LT19 KFN", "hours": 4, "permit": False, "height": 1.4},
    {"reg": "YB70 XCE", "hours": 6, "permit": False, "height": 2.4},
    {"reg": "PK21 RAV", "hours": 1, "permit": False, "height": 1.6},
]
takings = 0
refused_count = 0
largest_owed = 0
largest_reg = ""
for car in cars:
    if car["height"] > 2:
        refused_count += 1
        print(f'{car["reg"]}: refused — coach park')
        continue
    owed = 0
    if not car["permit"] and car["hours"] > 1:
        owed = (car["hours"] - 1) * 2
    takings += owed
    if owed > largest_owed:
        largest_owed = owed
        largest_reg = car["reg"]
    print(f'{car["reg"]}: {car["hours"]} hours, £{owed:.2f}')

Choice B — keyed by registration

Job 7 is direct: cars["LT19 KFN"]. The price is that building the data takes a little more deliberate naming.

Whichever shape you chose, you needed the same questions; only the route to a later answer changed.

Try these before moving on

Break it deliberately What you should notice
Use elif for refusal and charging The second independent question is silently skipped.
Charge hours * 2 The free hour disappears.
Add a refused car to takings The total is wrong despite the refusal message looking right.
Use :.2f on text Python objects: formatting belongs to the money number.
